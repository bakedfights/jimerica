      How to stream

      1. Use a Spotify account and stay signed in. Free and Premium streams both count; Premium lets you pick tracks freely.
      2. Play the full song. A play counts after about 30 seconds, but listening through is the natural way to support him.
      3. Turn the volume up and keep the app active. Muted or silent plays may not count.
      4. Mix in other songs. Make playlists that blend Jimin songs with other artists instead of looping one track nonstop.
      5. Save and follow. Like his songs, save the albums and follow his artist profile.
      6. Share links. Send Spotify links to friends; new listeners make the biggest difference.
