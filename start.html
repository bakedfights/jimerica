    New to JIMERICA? Here is how the stats work and how to support Jimin on Spotify.

    How the stats work

    • Where the numbers come from. Every stream count on JIMERICA comes from Spotify: each song's all-time play count, plus Spotify's daily country charts for "WHO". Other apps are not counted.
    • Updated every day. The site refreshes once each morning, Pacific time. Each refresh shows the numbers for the day before, because a full day of Spotify streams has to finish first.
    • Daily gains. A song's daily gain is how many streams it added in one day: its total that day minus its total the day before. The Overview ranks the biggest daily gains and shows whether each song went up or down from the previous day.
    • "Data as of" and "Data updated". "Data as of" is the day the Spotify numbers belong to. "Data updated", the badge at the top of each page, is the day the site was last refreshed. They are usually one day apart.
    • Monthly listeners. The number of people who played Jimin on Spotify in the last 28 days, as shown on his Spotify artist profile. It is a rolling count, not a running total, so it can go down as well as up.

    What milestones are

    • A milestone is a round number of all-time streams that a song is about to pass, like 100M, 500M or 1B. The steps grow with the song: every 10M up to 100M, every 50M up to 1B, then every 100M.
    • Dates are estimates. Each estimated date assumes the song keeps its average daily streams from the last 7 days. They are not predictions or official figures, and they move when streaming picks up or slows down.
    • Where to find them. The Next milestones table on the Overview lists the closest ones, and every song page shows that song's next milestone.

    Streaming tips

    1. Use a Spotify account and stay signed in. Free and Premium streams both count; Premium lets you pick tracks freely.
    2. Play the full song. A play counts after about 30 seconds, but listening through is the natural way to support him.
    3. Turn the volume up and keep the app active. Muted or silent plays may not count.
    4. Mix in other songs. Make playlists that blend Jimin songs with other artists instead of looping one track nonstop.
    5. Save and follow. Like his songs, save the albums and follow his artist profile.
    6. Share links. Send Spotify links to friends; new listeners make the biggest difference.

    Follow Spotify's terms of use. Bots, fake accounts and paid streams are filtered out and can hurt the artist.
