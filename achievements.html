    Chart records & certifications

    FACE (March 24, 2023)

    AchievementSource
    “Like Crazy” debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, the first solo No. 1 for a BTS member and the first for a South Korean soloistbillboard.com ↗
    FACE debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 with 164,000 units, the highest-charting album by a Korean soloist at the timebillboard.com ↗
    “Like Crazy” sold 254,000 downloads and CD singles in its first week, the biggest single-week sales total of 2023 at that pointbillboard.com ↗
    Sold over 1.45 million copies in its first week, the first solo artist in Hanteo history to pass that mark; No. 1 on the Circle Album ChartWikipedia (Face) ↗
    No. 1 on Oricon's Albums and Digital Albums chartsWikipedia (Face) ↗
    Certifications: KMCA Million; RIAJ Platinum; “Like Crazy” RIAA Platinum and Music Canada PlatinumWikipedia (discography) ↗
    Guinness World Record: fastest male K-pop soloist to reach 1 billion Spotify streams (393 days, May 22, 2023)guinnessworldrecords.com ↗

    MUSE (July 19, 2024)

    AchievementSource
    Debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 with 96,000 units, his second No. 2 debutWikipedia (Muse) ↗
    Sold 771,129 copies in its first week, debuting at No. 2 on the Circle Album Chart; No. 3 on OriconWikipedia (Muse) ↗
    “Who” debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard Global 200 and Global Excl. US and held both spots for two weeksWikipedia (Who) ↗
    “Who” peaked at No. 12 on the Hot 100 and charted for 33 weeks, then the longest run by a K-pop actWikipedia (Who) ↗
    “Who” reached No. 1 on Spotify's Global Daily chart and No. 4 in the UKWikipedia (Who) ↗
    “Who” passed 2.5 billion Spotify streams, the first solo song by a Korean artist with no collaborators to reach thatstarnewskorea.com ↗
    MUSE spent 34 weeks on the Billboard 200, the longest-charting K-pop solo albumajupress.com ↗
    Certifications: MUSE KMCA 2× Platinum and RIAJ Gold; “Who” BPI Gold, Music Canada Platinum and RIAJ Platinum (streaming)Wikipedia (discography) ↗
