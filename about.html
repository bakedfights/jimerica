Biography

Park Ji-min (박지민), known as Jimin, is a South Korean singer, songwriter and dancer, born on October 13, 1995, in Busan. He studied contemporary dance at Busan High School of Arts, joined Big Hit Entertainment after passing auditions in 2012, and graduated from Korean Arts High School in 2014.

He debuted as a member of BTS in 2013. Under BTS's name he released the solo tracks "Lie" (2016), "Serendipity" (2017) and "Filter" (2020), and his first credited solo song, "Promise", came out on December 30, 2018.

His first solo album, FACE, was released on March 24, 2023; its single "Like Crazy" became the first Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 by a South Korean solo artist. His second album, MUSE, followed on July 19, 2024, led by "Who".

Jimin enlisted for mandatory military service on December 12, 2023, serving as an active-duty soldier in an artillery unit of the Army 5th Infantry Division, and was discharged on June 11, 2025.
